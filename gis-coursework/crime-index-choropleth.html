GIS Coursework / GIS II / US Crime Index Map

US Crime Index Map

Overview

Built a choropleth map of a national crime index using Esri Business Analyst, comparing regional patterns like a high-crime band along the southern Mississippi border.

Process

Used Business Analyst’s built-in data and mapping tools to symbolize the crime index by county — an alternative workflow to the standard ArcGIS Pro choropleth method.
